# DMIT – Seedha Sawal (Clear Questions)

Bas in ke jawab de do. Ek line ya haan/naa kaafi hai.

---

## Beneficiary (Dusre ke liye booking)

**1.** Jab user apni wife/bachche/bhai ke liye DMIT book kare, to us person ka **phone number** zaroori hona chahiye?

- **Jawab: Haan**

---

**2.** Agar Child (minor) ka phone nahi hai, to kya parent ka phone use karenge lock ke liye?

- **Jawab: Haan**

---

**3.** Same person do alag users ne book kare (jaise Husband aur Wife dono ne apne bacche ke liye) – system ko kaise pata chalega ki ye same baccha hai?

- **Jawab: Pata nahi chalega (allow duplicate)**

---

## Batch booking (3 orders sath)

**4.** "3 order sath create kar du" – matlab: **ek hi date, ek hi time slot** par Trainer ko 3 alag sessions (mera, wife ka, papa ka)?

- **Jawab: Haan kar sakta hai**

---

**5.** Booking form mein "For whom" kaise dikhe?

- **Jawab: B)** Sirf "Add person" – name + phone type karo

---

## Admin bypass

**6.** Jab user bolta hai "meri finger analysis vapas kro" – Admin kya karega?

- **Jawab: Naya appointment manually create kare**

---

**7.** Admin ye bypass kitni baar kar sakta hai – unlimited ya limit?

- **Jawab: Unlimited**

---

## Free session expiry

**8.** Free sessions kab expire Honge?

- **Jawab:** Registration ke X mahine baad – Admin set karega (jaise 2 mahine to jo user aa register hua us din se next 2 months)

---

**9.** User ko kya dikhe?

- **Jawab: Haan** – "Aapke paas 2 free sessions hain, use 31 Dec 2025 tak" – ye sahi format

---

## Geographic / Downline

**10.** "Nearby Trainers" – Nearby ka matlab?

- **Jawab: Skip** – Location data project mein nahi hai. Agar available ho to dikha do.

---

**11.** "Same downline first" – matlab?

- **Jawab: All** – Direct referral + uske niche sab levels

---

Bas. In 11 sawalon ke jawab de do.
